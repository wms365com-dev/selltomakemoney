# Customer Shopping Experience Checklist

Goal: make the site feel simple, trustworthy, and fast for first-time shoppers.

## Fix first

- [x] Show products quickly on mobile instead of making shoppers scroll through intro content first.
- [x] Group products by department so the storefront feels easier to browse.
- [x] Keep search visible and easy to use.
- [x] Keep clear `Add to cart` and `Buy` actions on every product card.
- [x] Add real support and policy pages so shoppers can verify shipping, returns, and contact information.
- [x] Add a compact trust strip near the top of the storefront.
- [x] Make checkout wording clearer and more customer-friendly.
- [x] Make the checkout submit button reflect what happens next.
- [x] Add a clear empty-cart state with a way back to shopping.

## Fix next

- [ ] Add a compact “Need help?” block near product CTAs on cards or product pages.
- [ ] Show estimated shipping or pickup timing more clearly on product pages.
- [ ] Add product-review style trust signals where appropriate.
- [ ] Reduce oversized category counts like `Other` by splitting weak categories into cleaner departments.
- [ ] Improve product titles and category naming consistency.
- [ ] Add cleaner cart progress feedback after quantity changes.
- [ ] Add a lightweight “recently viewed” or “continue browsing” rail.

## Fix later

- [ ] Add better product filters beyond category and search.
- [ ] Add smarter recommendations by department.
- [ ] Add location-aware store switching for Canada vs USA.
- [ ] Add stronger order confirmation and post-checkout reassurance.
- [ ] Add more polished transactional email or SMS follow-up after checkout.

## What “simple shopping” means here

1. Shoppers understand what the site sells within seconds.
2. They can search or browse departments immediately.
3. They can compare options without scrolling through clutter.
4. They know whether the item is pickup-only or can ship.
5. They trust the business enough to enter checkout details.
6. They understand what happens after they submit or pay.
